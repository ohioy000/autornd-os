### 2. Hydraulic Pressure Drop with Glycol Coolants
Catalogue resistance coefficients $K_{\text{hyd}}$ are calibrated using pure deionized water at $25\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ ($\rho_{\text{ref}} = 997\text{ kg/m}^3$, $\nu_{\text{ref}} = 0.890 \times 10^{-6}\text{ m}^2/\text{s}$). When operating with aqueous glycol solutions at volumetric flow rate $Q_{\text{Lpm}}$ (in $\text{L/min}$), the total assembly pressure drop $\Delta P_{\text{total}}$ (in kPa) is the sum of the viscosity-corrected fin-core drop and the turbulent manifold port loss:
$$\Delta P_{\text{core}} = K_{\text{hyd}} \left(\frac{\rho}{\rho_{\text{ref}}}\right) \left(\frac{\nu}{\nu_{\text{ref}}}\right)^{0.25} Q_{\text{Lpm}}^{1.75}$$
$$\Delta P_{\text{port}} = K_{\text{port}} \left(\frac{\rho}{1000}\right) Q_{\text{Lpm}}^2$$
$$\Delta P_{\text{total}} = \Delta P_{\text{core}} + \Delta P_{\text{port}}$$
where $\rho$ is the coolant density ($\text{kg/m}^3$) and $\nu = \mu / \rho$ is the coolant kinematic viscosity ($\text{m}^2/\text{s}$).

### 3. Thermal Modeling & Derating Rules

#### 3.1 Series-Serpentine Coolant Temperature Reference
In all TVC-Series plates, the coolant flows sequentially beneath Module 1 (upstream) and Module 2 (downstream). Because internal fin conduction mixes the thermal boundary layer toward the exit of each zone, the peak baseplate temperature occurs at the trailing edge of **Module 2** and must be referenced to the **coolant outlet temperature** $T_{\text{out}}$:
$$T_{\text{out}} = T_{\text{in}} + \frac{P_{\text{total}}}{\dot{m} \cdot c_p}$$

#### 3.2 Glycol Convective & Lateral Footprint Constriction Multipliers
Catalogue thermal resistance $R_{\text{th,cat}}$ (at $Q_{\text{Lpm}} = 6.00\text{ L/min}$) is measured with pure water and a full-width heat source ($W_{\text{mod}} = W_f$). For actual applications, the effective cold-plate convective thermal resistance per module, $R_{\text{conv}}$, is:
$$R_{\text{conv}} = R_{\text{th,cat}} \cdot M_s \cdot F_{\text{gly}}$$
1. **Lateral Footprint Constriction Factor ($M_s$):** When the heat source width $W_{\text{mod}}$ (transverse to flow) is smaller than the active fin width $W_f$, fin-efficiency drop across the unloaded lateral channels increases resistance by:
   $$M_s = \left(\frac{W_f}{W_{\text{mod}}}\right)^{0.35}$$
2. **Glycol Convective Factor ($F_{\text{gly}}$):**
   - Pure water: $F_{\text{gly}} = 1.00$
   - 30/70 Ethylene Glycol / Water: $F_{\text{gly}} = 1.14$
   - 50/50 Ethylene Glycol / Water: $F_{\text{gly}} = 1.28$

#### 3.3 Peak Module Baseplate Temperature ($T_{b,\max}$)
With a thermal interface material (TIM) of areal resistance $R''_{\text{tim}}$ ($\text{K}\cdot\text{cm}^2/\text{W}$) over module footprint area $A_{\text{mod}}$ ($\text{cm}^2$), the TIM resistance per module is $R_{\text{tim}} = R''_{\text{tim}} / A_{\text{mod}}$. The peak baseplate temperature (Module 2) is:
$$T_{b,\max} = T_{\text{out}} + P_{\text{mod}} \cdot (R_{\text{tim}} + R_{\text{conv}})$$
