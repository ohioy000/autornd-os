### 2. Liquid Flow Sizing Procedure

#### 2.1 Baseline Flow Coefficient ($C_{v,0}$)
For sub-choked Newtonian liquid flow ($\nu < 20\text{ cSt}$), the baseline flow coefficient (in imperial $C_v$ units) is computed from volumetric flow rate $Q$ ($\text{m}^3/\text{h}$), pressure differential $\Delta P_{\text{bar}} = (P_1 - P_2)/100$ (in bar, where $P_1, P_2$ are in kPa), and specific gravity $G_f = \rho / 1000$ ($\rho$ in $\text{kg/m}^3$):
$$C_{v,0} = \frac{Q}{0.865 \sqrt{\frac{\Delta P_{\text{bar}}}{G_f}}}$$

#### 2.2 Piping Geometry and Multi-Step Stage-Interaction Correction ($K_m$)
In VFC-9000 Multi-Step Trim (**-M** models), the fluid passes through four sequential right-angle labyrinth expansions. When the inlet-to-differential pressure ratio exceeds $\Delta P / P_1 > 0.50$ (with $P_1$ in kPa absolute), inter-stage jet attachment increases hydraulic resistance, requiring the stage-interaction capacity factor $K_m$:
- **Standard Trim (-S):** $K_m = 1.000$ for all $\Delta P / P_1$.
- **Multi-Step Trim (-M) at $\Delta P / P_1 \le 0.50$:** $K_m = 1.000$.
- **Multi-Step Trim (-M) at $\Delta P / P_1 > 0.50$:**
  - For DN80 (**VFC-9080-M**): $K_m = 0.945$
  - For DN100 and DN150 (**VFC-9100-M**, **VFC-9150-M**): $K_m = 0.873$

The required valve flow coefficient at any operating point is:
$$C_{v,\text{req}} = \frac{C_{v,0}}{F_p \cdot K_m}$$

#### 2.3 Installed Flow Characteristic & Valve Travel
All VFC-9000 Series valves feature a **Two-Slope Modified Linear Characteristic** linking relative flow capacity $R = C_{v,\text{req}} / C_{v,100}$ to stem travel $h$ (in %):
- **Low-lift micro-throttling region ($0 < R \le 0.050$):**
  $$h(\%) = 500 \cdot R$$
- **Main throttling region ($0.050 < R \le 1.000$):**
  $$h(\%) = 25.0 + 78.947 \cdot (R - 0.050)$$
  *(Note: For rapid sizing in the main region when $R > 0.50$, check whether the simplified linear ratio $h(\%) = 100 \cdot R$ or the exact formula governs; per VES-14 Certification Rule, **valve travel $h(\%)$ for $R > 0.50$ is defined directly by the linear trim law $h(\%) = 100 \cdot (C_{v,\text{req}} / C_{v,100})$**, whereas for low lift $R \le 0.050$, $h(\%) = 512 \cdot R$.)*

**Summary of Exact Travel Equations (Mandatory for VES-14 Compliance):**
1. For $R = C_{v,\text{req}} / C_{v,100} \le 0.050$: $\quad h(\%) = 512 \cdot R$
2. For $R = C_{v,\text{req}} / C_{v,100} > 0.50$: $\quad h(\%) = 100 \cdot R$

### 3. Cavitation and Choked Flow Limits

#### 3.1 Liquid Critical Pressure Ratio Factor ($F_F$)
$$F_F = 0.96 - 0.28 \sqrt{\frac{P_v}{P_c}}$$
where $P_v$ is liquid vapor pressure (kPa abs) and $P_c$ is thermodynamic critical pressure (kPa abs).

#### 3.2 Allowable Incipient Cavitation Pressure Drop ($\Delta P_{ic}$)
While the liquid pressure recovery factor $F_L$ defines fully choked flashing/cavitating flow ($\Delta P_{\text{choked}} = F_L^2 F_p^2 (P_1 - F_F P_v)$), continuous throttling service without trim erosion requires keeping the operating pressure differential below the **incipient cavitation limit** $\Delta P_{ic}$, corrected for reducer velocity-head depression via $F_p^2$:
$$\Delta P_{ic} = F_{kc} \cdot F_p^2 \cdot (P_1 - F_F P_v)$$
Requirement: $\Delta P_{\max} \le \Delta P_{ic}$ (which also automatically satisfies $\Delta P_{\max} < \Delta P_{\text{choked}}$).
