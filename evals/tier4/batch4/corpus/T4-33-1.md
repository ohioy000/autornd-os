# Pellam Process Pumps
## PP series
Catalogue Q_cat, H_cat, P_cat and NPSHr are water ratings at the published speed and at the stated duty point. Do not apply a generic viscosity chart or an affinity-law speed change in place of the factors below.

### Rules for viscosity 100 to 250 mm²/s inclusive
1. Cq = 0.82, Ch = 0.90, Ceff = 0.70.
2. Delivered flow Q_del = Cq * Q_cat. Delivered head H_del = Ch * H_cat.
3. Shaft power P_shaft = P_cat * specific_gravity / Ceff.
4. NPSHr_visc = 1.15 * NPSHr. Available NPSH must be at least NPSHr_visc + 0.5 m.
5. Accept only if Q_del and H_del meet the duty, P_shaft does not exceed available shaft power, and the NPSH inequality is met.
6. Choose the lowest price among accepted models.
