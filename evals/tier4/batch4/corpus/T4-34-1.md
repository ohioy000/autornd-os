# Varmex Thermal
## VX extruded heatsinks
R_cat is the sink-to-ambient thermal resistance in free air with fins vertical, still air, and dissipation from 20 W to 150 W. Do not recalculate R_cat from a convection correlation.

| Model | Price EUR | R_cat K/W | Length mm | Mass kg |
| VX-180 | 18 | 0.70 | 100 | 0.40 |
| VX-250 | 27 | 0.50 | 110 | 0.62 |
| VX-320 | 36 | 0.38 | 115 | 0.88 |
| VX-400 | 48 | 0.28 | 118 | 1.15 |

### Rules
1. Required resistance is (maximum base temperature - ambient temperature) / dissipation.
2. Horizontal mounting with fins facing up: multiply R_cat by 1.25. Vertical fins use 1.00. No other orientation factor is published.
3. The standard dry pad is always fitted on this series and adds 0.05 K/W after the orientation multiplier. R_sa = orientation_factor * R_cat + 0.05.
4. Accept only if R_sa is not greater than the required resistance, length is not greater than the available length, mass is not greater than the stated mass limit, and dissipation is from 20 W to 150 W inclusive.
5. Choose the lowest price among accepted models, then the lowest mass.
