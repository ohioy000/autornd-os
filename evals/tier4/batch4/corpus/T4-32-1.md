# Helion Controls
## HC contactors
Currents are valid at 40 °C ambient, open mounting, altitude up to 2000 m. Do not replace the factors below with a generic contactor derating curve.

### Rules
1. Direct-on-line switching of a squirrel-cage motor shall be checked against I_AC3, not against Ith and not against an AC-1 rating. The AC-3 value already covers a start up to 8 times full-load current for at most 10 s.
2. Above 40 °C, I_amb = I_AC3 * (1 - 0.015*(T_amb - 40)). Apply the same multiplier to Ith.
3. In an unventilated IP54 enclosure, multiply the ambient-derated current by 0.85. This enclosure factor is not included in the catalogue currents.
4. Derated Ith must be at least 1.25 times motor full-load current.
5. Ue must be at least the system voltage, and H_max must be at least the site altitude. No extra altitude factor applies at or below 2000 m.
6. Choose the lowest price among models that pass every rule.
