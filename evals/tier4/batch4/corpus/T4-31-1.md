# Nordling Drive Components
## ND-Flex servo couplings
T_cat is the nominal torque at coupling speed up to 3000 min⁻¹ and ambient up to 40 °C. It is not a selection torque. Do not replace these rules with a generic coupling service-factor table.

| Model | Price EUR | T_cat N·m | n_max min⁻¹ | alpha_max deg | Mass kg |
| ND-C20 | 85 | 40 | 6000 | 1.5 | 0.35 |
| ND-C35 | 120 | 70 | 5500 | 1.2 | 0.55 |
| ND-C50 | 165 | 110 | 4800 | 1.0 | 0.80 |
| ND-C80 | 210 | 180 | 4000 | 1.5 | 1.10 |

### Rules
1. Reversing servo duty with moderate shock: service factor Ks = 1.5.
2. If torque peaks occur more often than 10 times per hour, T_basis is the peak torque. Otherwise T_basis is the continuous torque. T_req = Ks * T_basis.
3. If speed n > 3000 min⁻¹, T_allow = T_cat * (1 - 0.0015*(n - 3000)). If n <= 3000, T_allow = T_cat. No separate ambient factor applies at or below 40 °C.
4. Accept a model only if T_allow >= T_req, n_max >= application speed, alpha_max >= application angular misalignment, and mass <= the stated mass limit.
5. Among accepted models choose the lowest price, then the lowest mass.
