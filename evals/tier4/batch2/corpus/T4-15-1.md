# Voss-Metra Structural Systems — VM-C Series Cold-Formed Channels
## Document VM-ENG-204 (Rev 4): Catalogue Properties & Racking Strut Design Rules

### 1. Geometric Properties of VM-C Lipped Channels
All VM-C profiles are roll-formed from Grade S355 structural strip ($f_y = 355\text{ MPa}$, $E = 210\text{ GPa}$, $G = 81\text{ GPa}$) with single axis of symmetry ($x$-axis is the axis of symmetry / major axis; $y$-axis is the minor axis parallel to the web). Because of the thick lip and corner geometry, all four standard sections are Class 1–3 under uniform compression at $f_y = 355\text{ MPa}$ ($A_{eff} = A$).

| Section | Gross Area $A$ ($\text{mm}^2$) | Major-Axis $I_x$ ($10^6\text{ mm}^4$) | Minor-Axis $I_y$ ($10^6\text{ mm}^4$) | St. Venant Torsion $J$ ($\text{mm}^4$) | Warping Constant $I_w$ ($10^9\text{ mm}^6$) | Shear-Centre Distance $x_0$ ($\text{mm}$) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **VM-C140** | 620 | 1.950 | 0.580 | 640 | 2.40 | 42.0 |
| **VM-C160** | 800 | 3.200 | 0.923 | 1067 | 5.30 | 49.0 |
| **VM-C180** | 1080 | 5.450 | 1.620 | 2590 | 13.50 | 55.4 |
| **VM-C200** | 1350 | 8.200 | 2.404 | 4500 | 28.50 | 62.0 |

*Note*: $x_0$ is the distance along the $x$-axis between the gross centroid $C$ and the shear centre $S$ ($y_0 = 0$ by symmetry).

### 2. Maker Application Rules for Automated Racking Towers
When VM-C sections are used as primary compression struts in automated racking towers under EN 1993-1-1 / EN 1993-1-3:
1. **Buckling Curve**: Cold-formed lipped channels in the VM-C Series shall be designed using Eurocode buckling curve **b** (imperfection factor $\alpha = 0.34$) for all buckling modes (flexural, torsional, and torsional-flexural).
2. **Slenderness Limit**: To limit dynamic sway and shuttle alignment tolerances, the governing non-dimensional slenderness $\bar{\lambda}$ computed from the lowest elastic critical buckling load $N_{cr} = \min(N_{cr,x}, N_{cr,y}, N_{cr,TF})$ shall not exceed:
   $$\bar{\lambda}_{\max} = 1.30$$
3. **Unbraced Twist Knock-Down Factor ($k_{VM}$)**: Because open cold-formed channels with long torsional spans are sensitive to initial lip-waviness interaction with warping torsion, the factored design buckling resistance $N_{b,Rd}$ shall include the Voss-Metra knock-down factor $k_{VM}$:
   - For torsional effective length $L_{ez} \le 2.50\text{ m}$: $k_{VM} = 1.00$
   - For torsional effective length $L_{ez} > 2.50\text{ m}$: $k_{VM} = 0.92$
   such that $N_{b,Rd} = k_{VM} \chi A f_y / \gamma_{M1}$.
