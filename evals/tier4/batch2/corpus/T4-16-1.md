### 2. Impeller Trimming & Performance Scaling Laws
Impellers may be machine-trimmed in $1\text{ mm}$ integer increments down to $D_{\min} = 0.85 D_0$. For any trimmed diameter $D_{trim} \in [0.85 D_0, D_0]$:
1. **Head-Flow Curve**:
   $$H_{pump}(Q, D_{trim}) = H_0\left(\frac{D_{trim}}{D_0}\right)^2 - S_0 Q^2$$
   *(where $Q$ is in $\text{m}^3/\text{h}$ and $H_{pump}$ is in $\text{m}$)*.
2. **Best Efficiency Point (BEP) Shift and Preferred Operating Region (POR)**:
   $$Q_{BEP,trim} = Q_{BEP0}\left(\frac{D_{trim}}{D_0}\right)$$
   Continuous operation is permitted only when the duty flow rate $Q$ lies within $[0.70\,Q_{BEP,trim},\; 1.15\,Q_{BEP,trim}]$.
3. **Pump Efficiency with Trim Penalty**:
   Trimming increases tip clearance and blade wake mixing, reducing peak efficiency by $\Delta \eta_{trim} = 0.25\left(1 - \frac{D_{trim}}{D_0}\right)$:
   $$\eta_{pump}(Q, D_{trim}) = \eta_{\max} - k_\eta\left(Q - Q_{BEP,trim}\right)^2 - 0.25\left(1 - \frac{D_{trim}}{D_0}\right)$$

### 3. Trim-Corrected Cavitation Rule ($\text{NPSH}_{R,corr}$)
Because trimming the outer diameter $D_0$ reduces blade loading at the periphery while forcing the eye to operate at higher relative incidence for a fixed discharge head, the required NPSH **increases** with impeller trim:
$$\text{NPSH}_{R,corr} = \text{NPSH}_{R0}\left(\frac{D_0}{D_{trim}}\right)^{1.5}$$
For all chemical brine duties, the available NPSH at the suction flange ($\text{NPSH}_A$) must satisfy:
$$\text{NPSH}_A \ge 1.15\,\text{NPSH}_{R,corr} + 0.50\text{ m}$$

### 4. Motor Thermal Derating in Chemical Enclosure Service
All KV-Chem pumps installed in pressurized reactor transfer loops use acoustic/chemical splash shrouds and variable-frequency drives; the maximum continuous shaft power $P_{shaft}$ must not exceed the derated motor output:
$$P_{motor,derated} = 0.88\,P_{motor,nameplate}$$
