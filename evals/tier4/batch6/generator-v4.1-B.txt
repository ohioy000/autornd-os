You are writing a benchmark of HARD engineering design questions for expert practitioners. The difficulty must come from engineering judgment: a physical effect, a code convention or a load case that a careful expert working in one pass misses. It must not come from arithmetic, and it must not come from a rule written in the material you give the answerer. Every question must be checkable by recomputation.

Produce exactly 4 candidates as one JSON array, all of family "F2".
- The request names a fictional product line and states the duty and the requirements.
- The catalogue data lives only in corpus pages you also write.
- The textbook physics and the design-code methods stay with the answerer, and so does the trap.

Each candidate is an object with these fields:

- family: "F2"
- domain: use these 4 domains, one each: mechanical, structural, fluid and electrical.

  - statement;

- reference_design: the Results values of the correct selection.
- worked_solution: the full derivation, every intermediate value shown.
- wrong_designs: 2 objects (values, failed requirement ids, why). The first is exactly the selection a solver makes when the trap is missed.
- corpus_pages: 1 to 2 markdown documents of fictional catalogue data. Invented firms and products only. Never textbook physics, and never a rule about the trap.

Rules:
1. Closed-world data: every number needed is in the request or the corpus pages.
2. The selection rule (lightest, cheapest, lowest power...) must pick a DIFFERENT option when the trap is missed. The option picked with the trap missed must fail at least one requirement under the correct method by at least 3%.
3. Under the correct method, at least one cheaper or lighter option fails; the reference passes every requirement by at least 3%. The option_table must show this.
4. Several standard methods may exist for a requirement. The verdicts in option_table must not depend on which standard method is used; say which methods you checked.
   - Where design codes differ, choose the data so every option's verdict is the same under all of them, with at least 3% margin. Examples: column buckling curves, fatigue curves, load or resistance factors.
   - A section, material or class that selects a code curve must be stated as data.
5. No trick wording, no ambiguity, no missing or contradictory data. The corpus's notation must be self-consistent and defined.
6. Before writing each candidate, compute the whole option_table. A candidate whose table does not satisfy rules 2 and 3 is invalid: fix it before writing it.
7. Output the JSON array only, once. No commentary, no drafts, no restarts.
8. The trap must come from the answerer's own engineering knowledge, never from the corpus or the request.
   - No sentence in the request or the corpus may state the trap, name it, warn about it or hint at it.
   - The corpus holds no maker's rule that, applied literally, avoids the trap.
   - A trap that is a derating, selection or application rule written in the corpus is invalid.
9. Draw each trap from one of these sources, and use at least 3 different sources across the 4 candidates.
   - notation: the catalogue's notation differs from the standard method's. Examples: axis labels, a range where the method needs an amplitude, peak where it needs RMS, gauge where it needs absolute, a per-unit value where it needs a total. The corpus defines its own notation consistently and never compares it with the standard's.
   - reference condition: the catalogue states a quantity at a reference condition, and physics, not a maker's rule, changes it at the duty condition. Examples: fluid density, viscosity or vapour pressure at temperature; air density at altitude; resistance at temperature. The property data is given plainly as data.
   - implied load case: the duty implies a load or effect the request does not name. Examples: starting or accelerating load, inertia reflected through a ratio, restrained thermal expansion, eccentricity, a support condition's effective length.
   - governing mode: the obvious check passes, but another mode governs. Examples: stability, deflection, fatigue, resonance.
10. The trap must be fair. An expert who notices it agrees on the correct method. It rests on standard physics or a widely used code convention, never on an obscure clause or on a coefficient that is not given. Data the correct method needs is given plainly as data, never as an instruction.
11. The Results rows name neutral quantities.

   - A row that a solver fills differently when the trap is missed is good, but its name must not say which way.
