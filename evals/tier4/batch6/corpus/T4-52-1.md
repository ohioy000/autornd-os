# Helion Motion — Arc geared servos

Integral gearbox and servo motor. Ratio N is motor-shaft revolutions per output revolution. Torque ratings are at the motor shaft. Jm is the motor rotor inertia. Output rating is the torque limit at the gearbox output shaft. Maximum speed is motor-shaft speed.

| A80 | 40 | 0.00040 | 2.20 | 5.00 | 5000 | 120 | 880 |

Peak torque may be used for the acceleration interval. Continuous torque is the thermal rms limit. Gearing loss is not included in the ratings.
